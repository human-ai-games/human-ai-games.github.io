Games designed in the study

These are games designed by participants working with an AI coding agent in Conlon and Schwardmann, “Creative destruction: Human-AI competition in an experimental market.” Each producer was asked how much they would pay to have their game included on this website; the games shown here are those whose producers paid for inclusion. Each game starts from one of seven basic games. Consumer WTP is what consumers in the study were willing to pay, in cents, to play the game rather than the basic game it started from (for professionals' games, the average across consumers). Press Start to play.
